What is EMI?

Equated Monthly Instalment (EMI) is the fixed monthly amount a borrower pays to repay a loan over an agreed tenure. For a standard fixed-rate reducing-balance loan, each instalment contains an interest component and a principal component. The interest portion generally falls as the outstanding principal reduces.

The standard EMI formula is:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)

Here, P is the loan principal, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate divided by 12 and 100), and n is the total number of monthly instalments. When the interest rate is zero, EMI is simply principal divided by tenure in months.

EMI calculator for different loan types

Home loans usually have longer tenures, while car and personal loans often have shorter repayment periods. Education, gold and business loans can have different repayment structures, moratoriums, collateral requirements or fees. Frequently asked questions

How is EMI calculated?

For a standard fixed-rate reducing-balance loan, EMI is calculated using the principal, monthly interest rate and number of monthly instalments. The formula is P × r × (1+r)^n ÷ ((1+r)^n−1).

Can I calculate home, car and personal loan EMI?

Yes. Choose home, car, personal, education, gold, business or other loan. Each option fills an illustrative starting rate and tenure. Replace these with the terms offered by your lender.

Does making an extra monthly payment reduce interest?

Usually, paying extra towards principal reduces the outstanding balance faster and can lower interest. This calculator assumes the regular EMI stays unchanged and the extra amount is applied to principal after the monthly instalment. Lender rules and prepayment charges may differ.

Does EMI include processing fees and insurance?

No. EMI is calculated on principal and interest. The optional processing fee is shown separately as an upfront estimate. Insurance, stamp duty, taxes and other lender charges are not included.

Are the preset interest rates current bank offers?

No. Preset rates are illustrative starting values, not live bank quotes or guaranteed offers. Enter the annual rate from your lender's sanction letter or quotation.

Can I use this calculator for a floating-rate loan?

You can estimate EMI using the rate entered, but the calculator does not forecast future rate changes. If your lender changes the floating rate, recalculate using the revised rate and remaining tenure or the lender's revised repayment schedule.
