Job Offer Comparison FAQs

Should I compare job offers only by CTC?

No. Separate guaranteed fixed cash from target or conditional variable pay, one-time joining or retention bonuses, employer contributions, insurance and equity. Headline CTC may include items that are not monthly cash or guaranteed.

Is target variable pay guaranteed?

Usually not. Actual payout can depend on company, team and individual performance, plan rules and eligibility. Enter the guaranteed portion separately and use the attainment control only as a scenario assumption.

Does this calculator estimate income tax or take-home salary?

No. It compares gross cash compensation and user-entered living costs before income tax and payroll deductions. Use the CTC to In-Hand calculator separately for an estimated take-home figure.

How should I value ESOPs or stock awards?

Treat equity as a separate, uncertain component. Its value can depend on vesting, liquidity, share price, exercise price, tax, dilution and plan terms. This calculator displays the annual value you enter but does not treat it as guaranteed cash.
