This model assumes variable pay, bonus, employer PF, gratuity provision, insurance and employer NPS are included within the stated CTC. How salary breakup is calculated

The calculator starts with annual CTC, removes the variable pay, bonus and employer benefits you enter, then allocates the remaining fixed cash budget across basic salary, HRA and special allowance. Employer PF, gratuity provision and employer NPS are then included as employer costs when selected.

  • Basic salary: fixed gross cash × the basic percentage you select.
  • HRA: basic salary × the HRA percentage you select.
  • Special allowance: the remaining fixed gross cash after basic salary and HRA.
  • Employer PF: 12% of the modelled or overridden PF wage base, subject to the selected ceiling option.
  • Gratuity provision: 15/26 of the monthly modelled or overridden gratuity wage base for one year of service.
  • Estimated monthly cash: monthly gross cash less employee PF, professional tax and other deductions you enter. Income tax is not included.

Important: CTC structures vary by employer. The 50% statutory wage rule is modelled as a simplified minimum wage-base assumption; use the optional overrides for payroll-specific PF and gratuity wage figures. This tool does not determine statutory eligibility, income tax or the actual monthly payout schedule for variable pay and annual benefits.

Salary Breakup Calculator FAQs

What is included in a salary breakup?

A salary breakup explains how annual CTC is allocated across fixed cash salary such as basic pay, HRA and allowances, variable pay or bonus, employer contributions such as PF, gratuity provision and other benefits. The exact components depend on the employer's compensation policy.

Is basic salary a fixed percentage of CTC?

No single basic salary percentage applies to every employee or employer. The basic component is determined by the compensation structure and applicable wage rules. From 21 November 2025, the Labour Codes' wage definition also applies a 50% rule to specified wage components, so the statutory wage base used for PF and gratuity may be higher than basic salary alone.

Is employer PF part of CTC?

Where the employer includes its provident fund contribution in the stated CTC, it is part of the employer's total cost but is not paid to the employee as monthly cash salary. The Ministry's October 2026 FAQ states that PF contributions are 12% of wages and that the PF wage ceiling was revised to ₹25,000 per month by notification dated 17 September 2026. The actual PF wage base and coverage still depend on the employee's circumstances and payroll treatment.

Does this calculator show income-tax deductions?

No. It estimates the salary structure and a pre-income-tax monthly cash figure after employee PF and the professional tax amount you enter. Use the CTC to In-Hand calculator separately for an income-tax estimate.

What is the difference between gross salary and CTC?

Gross cash salary generally refers to salary components payable before employee deductions. CTC may also include employer PF, gratuity provision, insurance, variable pay and other employer costs, so CTC is not the same as monthly gross salary or take-home pay.

How is the statutory wage base estimated in this calculator?

By default, the calculator uses the higher of modelled basic salary or 50% of fixed gross as an estimate of statutory wages. This is a simplified model of the Labour Codes' wage definition. Enter the exact monthly PF or gratuity wage base from payroll in the optional override fields when available.

How is the gratuity provision estimated?

The calculator estimates an annual provision using 15 days' wages for each year of service, expressed as 15/26 of the monthly statutory wage base. It is a CTC allocation estimate, not a gratuity eligibility decision or final payout calculation. Actual gratuity depends on service conditions, statutory wage calculation and the ₹20 lakh ceiling.
