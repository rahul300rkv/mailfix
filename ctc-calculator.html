CTC, take-home salary and Tax Year 2026–27

This calculator converts an assumed CTC structure into an estimated monthly cash figure. It is a planning estimate, not a payslip, tax return or payroll determination. Match the inputs to your offer letter and employer's PF and benefit policy.

New regime

Tax Year 2026–27 slabs

₹0–4 lakh: nil; ₹4–8 lakh: 5%; ₹8–12 lakh: 10%; ₹12–16 lakh: 15%; ₹16–20 lakh: 20%; ₹20–24 lakh: 25%; above ₹24 lakh: 30%. Eligible resident individuals may receive a section 156 rebate up to ₹60,000, with marginal relief near ₹12 lakh.

Old regime

Age and deductions matter

For resident individuals, the basic exemption is generally ₹2.5 lakh below age 60, ₹3 lakh at 60–79 and ₹5 lakh at 80+. Eligible 80C, 80D and HRA claims can reduce taxable salary. The old-regime standard deduction is ₹50,000.

Salary math

Why CTC is not monthly cash

Employer PF and gratuity provision may be included in CTC but are not paid to your bank account as monthly salary. Variable pay, insurance, NPS, ESOPs and reimbursements can also change the actual cash pattern.

Assumptions

What this estimate simplifies

It assumes basic and HRA percentages supplied above, a 12% PF contribution on the modelled wage base, and gratuity provision of 4.81% of basic. It excludes surcharge, special-rate income, employer-specific benefits and payroll timing adjustments.

Official tax references

Read the Income Tax Department's 2026 memorandum for Tax Year 2026–27 rates and the Income-tax Act, 2025, as amended by Finance Act 2026 for the underlying provisions. For salary structure, compare the Salary Breakup Calculator and How CTC Is Calculated guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between CTC and in-hand salary?
CTC is the employer's total annual cost and may include employer PF, gratuity provision, insurance, variable pay and other benefits. In-hand salary is the cash left after employee-side PF, professional tax, income tax and other payroll deductions. The full CTC is not necessarily paid as monthly cash.
Which tax year and tax rates does this calculator use?
It estimates salary tax for Tax Year 2026–27 under the Income-tax Act, 2025. The new-regime slabs are nil up to ₹4 lakh, then 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 25% and 30% across successive ₹4 lakh bands. Eligible resident individuals may claim the section 156 rebate up to ₹60,000, subject to conditions and marginal relief. See the official tax-rate memorandum.
Does the new regime allow HRA, 80C and 80D deductions?
Generally, HRA exemption and deductions such as 80C and 80D are not available under the default new regime. This calculator applies those entered deductions only when Old Regime is selected. Professional tax and the applicable standard deduction are handled separately.
How does age affect old-regime tax?
For resident individuals, the old-regime basic exemption is generally ₹2.5 lakh below age 60, ₹3 lakh for senior citizens aged 60–79, and ₹5 lakh for super senior citizens aged 80 or above. Age does not change the new-regime slab thresholds. Non-residents do not receive the higher age-based basic exemption in the old regime.
Is PF mandatory for every employee?
PF coverage depends on establishment coverage, membership history, notified wage rules and the employee's circumstances. This calculator applies a simplified 12% employee and 12% employer contribution with a ₹25,000 monthly wage-base cap when PF is enabled. Actual payroll treatment may differ; confirm it with HR and EPFO.
How is HRA exemption estimated?
Under the old regime, the estimate uses the lowest of actual HRA received, rent paid minus 10% of salary, and 50% of salary for Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai or Kolkata (40% elsewhere). Detailed eligibility and salary definitions can vary; use the HRA exemption calculator for a more detailed estimate.
