8th Pay Commission DA and DR: Difference, Merger Questions and Calculation

Learn the difference between dearness allowance and dearness relief, and why future treatment must follow orders.

Quick answer: The 8th CPC review is in progress. Do not treat unofficial estimates as government decisions.

DA versus DR

Dearness allowance is the inflation-related component for serving employees. Dearness relief is the corresponding component for eligible pensioners. Both are governed by government orders.

What happens during pay revision

A new pay structure may require decisions on accumulated DA/DR, revised basic pay and future instalments. Do not assume a merger percentage or reset mechanism without an official order.

Why percentage alone is not enough

The rupee value depends on basic pay or pension and the rate applicable for each period. As of 25 November 2026, the Department of Expenditure order dated 29 October 2026 sets DA at 63% from 1 July 2026 for central civil employees under the 7th CPC structure; the PIB reports matching DR approval for pensioners. Armed Forces and Railway employees have separate orders. This current 7th CPC rate is not a forecast for the 8th CPC. See the Department of Expenditure order and PIB release.

Where to verify rates

Use Department of Expenditure and Department of Pension & Pensioners' Welfare orders for the relevant category. Confirm effective date and covered group.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between DA and DR?

DA generally applies to serving employees; DR is the corresponding inflation-linked component for eligible pensioners.

Will DA be merged into basic pay?

No merger is assumed. Follow the final government orders for the treatment of DA and DR.

Can a current DA rate be applied to every arrears month?

Not without checking the effective dates and rates applicable to each period.
