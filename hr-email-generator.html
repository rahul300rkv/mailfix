HR Email Generator FAQs

What HR emails can this tool write?

Delayed salary, pending salary after exit, final-month salary, early relieving, notice waiver, leave, appraisal, relieving letters, experience certificates, joining-date extensions, workplace concerns and follow-ups. You can also describe another HR request.

Can I generate an HR email in Hindi or Hinglish?

Yes. Choose English, Hindi or Hinglish as the output language. Review names, dates and facts before sending.

Does the generator make legal claims or send emails automatically?

No. It drafts text only and does not send messages or provide legal advice. It is instructed not to invent facts or make unsupported legal threats. Check the draft before sending.

What details should I include in an email to HR?

Include the reason for writing, relevant dates or salary period, a short factual summary and the specific action or response you need. Add only necessary details and avoid passwords, identity numbers, bank details or confidential company information.

Is my text stored by MailFix?

Your input is sent through MailFix's backend proxy to NVIDIA NIM for generation. MailFix's privacy policy states that AI input text is not stored on its servers. Review NVIDIA's privacy policy and avoid entering sensitive personal or confidential information.

Write a clearer HR email

A useful workplace email states the context, gives verifiable details and makes one clear request. Keep the tone professional even when following up about a delayed payment or an exit issue.

Before you send the draft

  • Check the recipient, subject, employee details, dates and salary period.
  • For salary or exit follow-ups, state the exact period and the action you are requesting.
  • For leave, early release or joining-date changes, include the dates and a practical handover plan where relevant.
  • Attach only documents the recipient needs, and use your organisation's approved channel for sensitive records.
  • Do not treat an AI-generated draft as confirmation of company policy or a legal entitlement. Verify those details before sending.
