PDF to image FAQs

Can I convert every page of a PDF to JPG?

Yes. The converter renders each page and packages the resulting JPG files into one ZIP download.

Can I convert PDF pages to PNG instead?

Yes. Choose PNG before converting. PNG is lossless and may produce larger files than JPG.

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

No. Conversion runs in your browser using JavaScript. Your selected PDF is not sent to MailFix.

Can I convert a scanned PDF?

Yes, if the PDF contains pages that can be rendered. Each rendered page is saved as an image; text will not become editable text.
