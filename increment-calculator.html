What is a good salary hike in India?

There is no universal “good hike” percentage. The result depends on the employer's annual salary budget, sector, role, performance rating, promotion, scarce skills and whether the move is an internal appraisal or a job change. Aon’s 2026–27 India survey projected an overall 9.1% salary increase for 2027, while its sector-level projections varied. Treat this as a broad employer survey benchmark, not an individual entitlement or a promise of what a particular company will pay.

For a meaningful comparison, look at fixed annual cash, target and paid variable pay, employer PF, gratuity, insurance, stock awards, joining bonus and work-related costs. A new offer with a higher CTC may still have lower recurring monthly cash if more of the package is variable or deferred.

Why CTC and take-home pay are different

Cost to Company may include employer contributions and benefits in addition to salary paid as cash. The calculator uses a simplified model: estimated cash gross is CTC less modelled employer PF and gratuity provision; employee PF, estimated income tax and the professional tax amount entered are then deducted to estimate monthly take-home. The basic-pay share and PF wage base are editable because employers structure compensation differently.

For a detailed breakup, compare this result with the CTC calculator, salary breakup calculator, salary slip calculator and CTC calculation guide. Check current income-tax slabs for the applicable rules.

Tax and PF assumptions for Tax Year 2026–27

The new-regime estimate uses the published slabs for Tax Year 2026–27: nil up to ₹4 lakh, then 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 25% and 30% across successive ₹4 lakh bands, with a ₹75,000 standard deduction for salary and 4% health and education cess. A resident individual rebate and marginal relief are applied around the ₹12 lakh taxable-income threshold in this simplified model. Surcharge and complex income items are not modelled.

The PF wage base defaults to ₹25,000 per month following the Ministry of Labour & Employment announcement effective 17 September 2026. This is not a statement that every employee's PF is necessarily capped at that figure: coverage and contribution rules depend on eligibility, existing membership, establishment and the employee's actual PF wage. Edit the field to match the PF wage base confirmed by payroll.

Salary increment calculator FAQs

How do I calculate a salary increment percentage?
Use (new annual CTC − current annual CTC) ÷ current annual CTC × 100. For example, a change from ₹8,00,000 to ₹9,60,000 is a ₹1,60,000 increase, or 20%. This compares CTC with CTC; it does not mean take-home pay rises by 20%.
What is the average salary hike in India for 2027?
Aon’s India Annual Salary Increase and Turnover Survey 2026–27 projected an overall 9.1% salary increase for 2027 across more than 1,200 organisations and 45 industries. This is a survey-based employer budget projection. Actual increments vary by sector, company, role, performance, promotion and location.
Why is my take-home increase lower than my CTC hike?
CTC can include employer PF, gratuity provision, insurance, variable pay and other benefits that are not paid as monthly cash. Employee PF, professional tax and income tax also affect take-home. Compare the salary components in both offer letters instead of applying the CTC percentage directly to monthly net pay.
How is the estimated in-hand salary calculated here?
The estimate starts with annual CTC, subtracts the modelled employer PF and gratuity provision to estimate recurring gross cash salary, applies the selected basic-pay share and PF wage base, deducts employee PF, estimated new-regime income tax and the monthly professional tax you enter. It uses the ₹75,000 standard deduction and 4% cess for Tax Year 2026–27. Actual payroll can differ.
What PF wage ceiling is used for the estimate?
The default is ₹25,000 per month, reflecting the wage-ceiling change announced as effective 17 September 2026. The PF wage base is editable because coverage, membership history, excluded allowances, employer policy and contributions on higher wages can change the actual calculation. Confirm your applicable PF wage with HR or EPFO.
Does the calculator support old tax regime and all tax deductions?
No. The take-home estimate is designed as an illustrative new-tax-regime calculation for Tax Year 2026–27. It does not model the old regime, every deduction or exemption, other income, special-rate income, surcharge or individual tax adjustments. Use the official Income Tax Department resources for a complete computation.
Is a 20% hike always better than a 15% hike?
Not necessarily. Compare fixed pay, variable pay, employer PF, gratuity, joining or retention bonus, benefits, work location, role scope and job security. A larger headline CTC can contain a larger variable component and may not produce a proportionate increase in recurring monthly pay.
How do I calculate the real salary hike after inflation?
A simple real-hike estimate is ((1 + nominal hike) ÷ (1 + inflation)) − 1. For example, a 10% nominal increase with 5% inflation is about a 4.76% real increase. This is a purchasing-power comparison, not a payroll or tax calculation.
