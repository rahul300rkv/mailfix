Gratuity Rules in India — Eligibility, Calculation Formula & Tax

Updated November 2026 · Covers the Code on Social Security, eligibility, calculation & tax treatment

Gratuity is included in your CTC as ~4.81% of basic — read how CTC is calculated for the full picture. When leaving a job also check your leave encashment payout and EPF withdrawal options.

Gratuity is a statutory terminal benefit paid to eligible employees. From 21 November 2025, the relevant gratuity provisions are governed by the Code on Social Security, 2020. Eligibility, the wage base and calculation can depend on service type, employment terms and statutory exceptions.

Quick summary: The general qualifying period is five years of continuous service, with exceptions including death, disablement and expiry of a fixed-term employment contract. Directly employed fixed-term employees qualify after one year under the contract. For a monthly-rated employee, an illustrative formula is last-drawn monthly gratuity wages × 15 × qualifying years ÷ 26. The current statutory maximum amount is ₹20 lakh; income-tax exemption is a separate matter.

Who is Eligible for Gratuity?

From 21 November 2025, gratuity is governed by the applicable provisions of the Code on Social Security, 2020. The general rule is five years of continuous service with the employer before gratuity becomes payable on resignation, retirement or termination.

  • The five-year condition does not apply in cases including death, disablement and expiry of a fixed-term employment contract.
  • A directly employed fixed-term employee becomes eligible after completing one year under the contract, as clarified by the Ministry of Labour and Employment.
  • For contract labour engaged through a contractor, the Ministry FAQ identifies the contractor as the employer responsible for gratuity after five years of continuous service.
  • Gratuity coverage generally applies to establishments covered by the Code; check the establishment's coverage and the applicable rules rather than assuming every engagement is covered identically.

Does the 4 Years 240 Days Rule Still Apply?

⚠️ Do not treat the 4 years and 240 days interpretation as a universal entitlement. The general qualifying period under the Code on Social Security, 2020 remains five years, subject to the statutory exceptions above. Earlier court decisions under the previous law may require case-specific legal interpretation.

Gratuity Calculation Formula

Under Section 53 of the Code on Social Security, 2020, gratuity for a monthly-rated employee is generally calculated using the last-drawn wages recognised for gratuity purposes:

Illustrative formula = Last-drawn monthly wages × 15 × qualifying years ÷ 26
The 15 represents 15 days' wages for each qualifying year; 26 is the working-day divisor used for monthly-rated employees. The wage base is not automatically just basic salary: the Code's definition of wages and its inclusions/exclusions, including the 50% allowance add-back rule where applicable, must be considered.

Gratuity is calculated for every completed year of service or part of a year in excess of six months. For example, 7 years and 8 months is generally counted as 8 years; 7 years and 4 months as 7 years. Fixed-term employees and deceased employees are covered by pro-rata provisions.

The Ministry has clarified that the revised wage definition applies to gratuity calculations from 21 November 2025. A basic-salary-only estimate may therefore be inaccurate where other wage components affect the statutory wage base.

Maximum Gratuity Limit

The Ministry of Labour and Employment currently states that the maximum gratuity amount notified by the Central Government is ₹20 lakh. Better gratuity terms may apply under an award, agreement or employment contract. Do not assume that the statutory maximum and the income-tax exemption limit are interchangeable.

Tax Treatment of Gratuity

Gratuity tax treatment is determined separately under the income-tax law applicable in the financial year in which the amount is received. The exemption can differ by employee category and is subject to category-specific conditions and limits.

⚠️ The statutory maximum gratuity amount of ₹20 lakh is not automatically the tax-exempt amount for every employee. Before relying on a tax estimate, verify the current income-tax provisions, employee category and exemption limit for the year of receipt. This guide does not calculate gratuity tax.

When Must the Employer Pay Gratuity?

Under the Code on Social Security, 2020, the employer must arrange payment of gratuity within the prescribed 30-day period after it becomes payable. Interest may apply when payment is delayed, subject to the statutory conditions.

If gratuity is withheld or disputed, retain the appointment letter, wage records, service dates and written communication, and seek guidance from the appropriate labour authority. The correct authority and procedure can depend on the establishment and the applicable rules.

Can Gratuity Be Forfeited?

Forfeiture is permitted only in the specific circumstances and to the extent set out in the Code on Social Security, 2020. It is not a general right to withhold gratuity because an employee resigns or leaves on unfavourable terms. Obtain case-specific advice before treating any amount as forfeited.

The statutory grounds include damage or loss caused by the employee's wilful act, omission or negligence, riotous or disorderly conduct or an act of violence, and an offence involving moral turpitude committed in the course of employment, subject to the Code's conditions.

Normal resignation, retirement, or performance-based exit does not forfeit gratuity. Only dismissal for proven misconduct qualifies. The forfeiture must be proportionate to the loss caused.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum service period to get gratuity?
The general qualifying period is five years of continuous service under the Code on Social Security, 2020. The five-year condition does not apply in cases including death, disablement and expiry of a fixed-term employment contract. A directly employed fixed-term employee is eligible after one year under the contract, according to Ministry of Labour and Employment FAQs. The 4 years and 240 days interpretation should not be treated as a universal entitlement.
What is the gratuity formula for private sector employees?
For a monthly-rated employee, an illustrative formula is last-drawn monthly gratuity wages × 15 × qualifying years of service ÷ 26. The wage base must be determined under the Code on Social Security, 2020, including the applicable definition of wages and the 50% allowance add-back rule. Service is counted for each completed year or part exceeding six months.
Is gratuity taxable in India?
Gratuity tax treatment is determined separately under the income-tax law applicable in the year of receipt. Exemption depends on employee category, statutory conditions and applicable limits. The statutory maximum gratuity amount of ₹20 lakh is not automatically the tax-exempt amount for every employee.
Can my employer refuse to pay gratuity?
An eligible employee has a statutory right to gratuity under the Code on Social Security, 2020, subject to the qualifying service rules and exceptions. If payment is refused or delayed, retain service and wage records and approach the appropriate authority under the applicable Code and rules. Interest may apply to delayed payment, subject to statutory conditions.
Is gratuity included in CTC?
Yes. Most employers include a gratuity provision of approximately 4.81% of basic salary per year in the CTC figure. This is because the employer is legally obligated to pay gratuity after 5 years. However, this provision is accrued internally and is only paid as a lump sum when you leave after completing 5+ years of service — it does not appear in your monthly payslip.
