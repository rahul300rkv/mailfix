Images to PDF FAQs

Can I combine multiple photos into one PDF?

Yes. Select multiple JPG, PNG or WebP images. Each image is placed on its own PDF page in the order shown in the list.

Can I change the order of images before converting?

Yes. Use the up and down controls beside each image to change the page sequence before creating the PDF.

Can I create an A4 PDF from mobile photos?

Yes. Choose A4 page size and portrait or landscape orientation. The image is scaled to fit within the selected margins without being stretched.

Are images uploaded to MailFix?

No. The PDF is created locally in your browser. Images are not sent to MailFix servers.
