How the rent vs buy comparison works

The buying scenario estimates the down payment, purchase costs, monthly EMI, maintenance and the home's future value. It subtracts the remaining loan and estimated selling costs to calculate equity.

The renting scenario invests the down payment and purchase costs that were not spent, then invests any monthly cash-flow advantage after rent and investment costs. Both scenarios use the same selected annual investment return.

Use realistic assumptions

Property prices and rents vary widely by city and neighbourhood. Use local quotes, include one-time charges and test more than one appreciation and return assumption before deciding.

Frequently asked questions

Is it better to rent or buy a house in India?

It depends on the purchase price, rent, loan rate, holding period, maintenance, property appreciation and the return you could earn by investing the down payment and monthly cost difference. Compare the estimated end-of-period net worth rather than EMI alone.

What is the break-even period for buying a house?

The break-even period is when the estimated financial value of buying catches up with renting and investing the money otherwise used for purchase. This calculator compares outcomes over your selected period; it is an estimate, not a guaranteed date.

Does this rent vs buy calculator include home loan interest?

Yes. It estimates loan EMI and the outstanding loan balance using a monthly reducing-balance calculation. Purchase costs, maintenance and property appreciation are also included in the comparison.

Why can renting be financially better than buying?

Renting may leave more money available to invest, avoids large transaction costs and reduces exposure to property-price or maintenance risk. Buying can become more attractive with longer holding periods or strong property appreciation.

What costs should I include when buying a house?

Consider down payment, stamp duty, registration, brokerage, loan processing, maintenance, repairs, property tax and eventual selling costs. Enter estimates for the costs that apply in your location.

Are the results guaranteed?

No. Rent increases, investment returns, property appreciation, loan rates, taxes and selling costs are uncertain. Change the assumptions to test conservative and optimistic scenarios.
