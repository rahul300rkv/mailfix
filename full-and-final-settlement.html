Full and Final Settlement FAQs

What is included in full and final settlement in India?

A final settlement may include unpaid salary, eligible leave encashment, gratuity where payable, approved incentives or reimbursements, and other contractual dues, less authorised recoveries such as notice pay, advances, loans and other deductions.

Is notice period recovery mandatory in every resignation?

Notice obligations and recovery are generally governed by the employment contract, applicable service rules and law. This calculator uses the shortfall days and salary basis you enter; it does not decide whether a recovery is legally or contractually valid.

Can I receive gratuity in my final settlement?

Gratuity may be payable on resignation or termination if the statutory eligibility conditions are met. The general continuous-service condition is five years, with statutory exceptions. A directly employed fixed-term employee is eligible after one year under the contract, as clarified by the Ministry of Labour and Employment.

How is leave encashment calculated in the final settlement?

A common estimate is eligible monthly leave-encashment wages divided by the employer's applicable divisor, multiplied by the eligible leave days. The wage base, divisor and entitlement depend on applicable law, worker classification, state rules and employment terms.

Is this final settlement amount guaranteed?

No. This is an estimate based on user-entered values and assumptions. The employer's final calculation may differ due to payroll cut-off, contract terms, statutory coverage, tax treatment, approved leave, wage definitions and authorised deductions.
