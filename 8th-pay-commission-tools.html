What is officially known?

The Government constituted the Eighth Central Pay Commission on 3 November 2025. The Cabinet-approved terms of reference provide an 18-month period for recommendations. The PIB background note says 1 January 2026 would normally be expected based on the ten-year convention; that statement is not itself an implementation order. A Lok Sabha answer dated 10 August 2026 said the Commission had not submitted recommendations as of that answer.

Primary sources: 8th CPC website · PIB Terms of Reference · Lok Sabha answer.
