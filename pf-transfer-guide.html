How to Transfer Your PF Account

UAN, Form 13, online steps, claim tracking and common problems when changing employers.

When you change jobs, your EPF membership generally continues under the same UAN. A transfer moves eligible PF accumulations from the previous Member ID to the current one and helps maintain your service record.

Before starting

  • Confirm the same UAN is linked to previous and current Member IDs. Resolve duplicate UANs with EPFO.
  • Check Aadhaar, name, date of birth, mobile and KYC details for mismatches.
  • Check that previous and current Member IDs, joining dates and exit dates appear correctly in service history.
  • Older EPFO guidance notes that the Member ID must be available in EPFO records and employer authorisation may be required in certain cases.

Online PF transfer steps

  1. Sign in to the EPFO Unified Member Portal using your UAN.
  2. Open Online Services or Service History and find the transfer request option (often “One Member – One EPF Account” or “Service Transfer Claim”). Labels may change.
  3. Review the previous and current Member IDs and select the previous account.
  4. Verify personal details, employment dates and destination account. Correct errors before submitting.
  5. Complete the authentication/OTP step shown and submit the transfer claim (commonly Form 13).
  6. Save the acknowledgement/reference number and track the claim in the portal.
  7. After processing, check passbook and service history for the transfer credit.
Revamped Form 13: The Ministry announced in April 2025 that destination-office approval was removed for the majority of transfer claims. This does not mean every claim is automatic; source-side checks and exceptions may still apply. Read the official PIB release.

Common issues

Previous Member ID not visible

Ask the previous employer whether the relevant ECR/returns were filed and whether the Member ID is available in EPFO records.

Name, DOB or Aadhaar mismatch

Compare UAN, Aadhaar and employer records. Follow the EPFO correction process and ask the employer to correct establishment-side details where needed.

Date of exit missing or incorrect

Check service history and ask the previous employer to update the exit date if it is not visible or correct. Use only the member-side update option if the current portal confirms eligibility.

Claim pending

Keep the acknowledgement, check claim status and contact the employer if a correction or action is pending. Escalate through EPFO's grievance mechanism if unresolved.

Exempted PF trust

Transfer procedures can differ for exempted establishments. Contact the trust and current employer and follow EPFO instructions for exempted accounts.

Multiple UANs

Do not submit repeated transfer claims blindly. Ask the employer and EPFO to resolve duplicate UAN records first.

Transfer or withdraw?

If you have joined another EPF-covered employer, transfer is generally the route to maintain continuity. Withdrawal is a separate claim with its own eligibility rules. A transfer is not a cash withdrawal and can help preserve service history relevant to pension benefits.

PF Transfer FAQs

Do I need a new UAN when changing jobs?

Normally, the same UAN is used for successive EPF-covered jobs. The new employer links the new Member ID to your existing UAN.

Is Form 13 still used?

Form 13 remains the transfer request framework. Follow the current portal's transfer option and instructions.

Does every claim need approval from both employers?

EPFO removed destination-office approval for the majority of claims, but some cases may still need employer or source-office action.
