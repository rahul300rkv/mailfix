How EPF, EPS and EDLI contributions are calculated

The calculator uses the monthly PF wage figure you enter, not CTC or total gross salary. Under the standard 12% assumption, the employee contributes 12% to EPF. The employer contributes 12% in total; where EPS applies, 8.33% of pensionable wages (up to the applicable ₹25,000 ceiling) is allocated to EPS and the balance goes to EPF. EDLI is shown separately as an estimated employer-side contribution at 0.5%, subject to the scheme rules.

EPF Calculator FAQs

What is the EPF wage ceiling in India in 2026?

The Central Government notified ₹25,000 per month as the wage ceiling for Chapter III of the Code on Social Security, 2020, effective 17 September 2026. Membership history and applicable scheme provisions still matter.

How much do employees and employers contribute to EPF?

The standard rate is generally 12% from the employee and 12% from the employer, subject to applicable establishment categories and scheme rules. Certain specified categories use 10%.

Is the employer's full 12% deposited into EPF?

Not always. For an eligible EPS member, 8.33% of pensionable wages is diverted to EPS, subject to the pensionable wage ceiling and scheme rules; the balance of the employer's share goes to EPF. If EPS does not apply, the employer's contribution is credited to EPF as per applicable rules.

Can an employee earning above ₹25,000 still have PF?

Yes. Existing EPF members generally continue membership when wages later exceed the ceiling. A person joining for the first time above the applicable ceiling may be excluded from mandatory membership if the scheme's exclusion conditions are met. Confirm your history and eligibility with HR or EPFO.

Does this calculator include PF interest?

No. It estimates contributions only. Actual balances depend on deposits, the interest rate declared for each financial year, posting dates, withdrawals and EPFO account records.

What changed when the EPF wage ceiling increased to ₹25,000?

From 17 September 2026, the mandatory-coverage wage ceiling increased from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 per month. Eligible employees previously excluded only because wages exceeded ₹15,000 may now be covered. The actual result depends on membership history, establishment coverage and scheme rules.

How is the employer's 12% divided between EPF and EPS?

For an eligible EPS member, 8.33% of pensionable wages up to the applicable ceiling is allocated to EPS; the remaining part of the employer's 12% goes to EPF. If EPS does not apply, the employer contribution is credited under the applicable EPF rules.

Does the calculator include VPF, interest or pension projections?

The higher employee contribution options are illustrative VPF scenarios. This tool estimates monthly and annual contributions only; it does not project EPF interest, account balances or monthly EPS pension. Check your passbook and EPFO records for actual balances.
