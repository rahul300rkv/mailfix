How CRS points are estimated

The score combines age, education, first and second official language, Canadian work experience, spouse factors, skill-transferability estimates and selected additional points. Real CRS calculations assess individual language abilities, credential equivalency, program eligibility and other detailed conditions.

For a formal assessment, use the official IRCC CRS score tool and review the official CRS points grid.

Frequently asked questions

What is the CRS score in Canada Express Entry?

The Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) is the points system IRCC uses to rank eligible Express Entry candidates. Points can depend on age, education, language ability, Canadian work experience, spouse factors and additional criteria.

What is the maximum CRS score?

The maximum score depends on whether a candidate is assessed with an accompanying spouse. Core human-capital and skill-transferability points have defined limits, and additional points may apply for factors such as provincial nomination or French-language ability. Always check the current IRCC CRS criteria.

How are IELTS scores converted to CRS points?

IRCC assesses approved language tests by individual ability and converts results to Canadian Language Benchmark (CLB) or Niveaux de compétence linguistique canadiens (NCLC) levels. IELTS General Training and IELTS Academic are not interchangeable for Express Entry.

Does a spouse affect my CRS score?

If your spouse or common-law partner accompanies you, some points are allocated to their education, language ability and Canadian work experience, while certain principal-applicant maxima differ. This calculator includes separate spouse inputs.

How many CRS points does a provincial nomination add?

An eligible provincial or territorial nomination through an Express Entry-aligned program generally adds 600 CRS points. Confirm the nomination stream and current IRCC rules before relying on this estimate.

Is this CRS calculator an official IRCC tool?

No. It is an independent estimator for planning. The official IRCC CRS tool and your Express Entry profile determine the applicable score under the rules in force when you apply.
