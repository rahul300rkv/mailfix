GST invoice essentials for Indian businesses

A tax invoice is a prescribed GST document, not just a bill with a tax percentage added. Confirm the supplier's registration, supply classification, place of supply, HSN/SAC and rate before issuing it.

Rule 46

Required invoice particulars

Supplier and recipient details, GSTIN where applicable, a unique serial number and date, HSN/SAC, description, quantity/UQC, taxable value, tax rates and amounts, place of supply where required, reverse-charge status and supplier authentication.

Tax split

CGST + SGST or IGST

For a domestic intra-state supply, the applicable GST is generally split equally between CGST and SGST/UTGST. For an inter-state supply, IGST generally applies. The place-of-supply rules decide the treatment.

HSN / SAC

Classify each item correctly

Use HSN for goods and SAC for services. GSTN's GSTR-1 Table 12 advisory generally requires at least 4 digits for preceding-year AATO up to ₹5 crore and 6 digits above ₹5 crore. Confirm the code and applicable invoice rules.

E-invoice

PDF is not an IRN e-invoice

Eligible taxpayers generally report covered documents to an authorised Invoice Registration Portal and obtain an IRN and signed QR code. This browser tool creates a printable draft; it does not report invoices to an IRP.

Current GST rate structure

India's rate structure was rationalised from 22 September 2025, with 5% and 18% as the two main rates and a special 40% rate for selected supplies. Classification-specific exemptions and other notified rates can still apply. Never select a rate from a generic list alone; verify the current CBIC notification for the item's HSN/SAC.

What this generator does not do

It does not generate an IRN or signed e-invoice QR code, file GSTR-1, create an e-way bill, calculate compensation cess, apply discounts, or handle exports, SEZ supplies and complex place-of-supply cases. Use your GST-compliant accounting system or authorised IRP where required. Review the full GST invoice guide before issuing a document.

GST invoice generator FAQs

Who can issue a GST tax invoice?
A registered person making a taxable supply is generally required to issue a tax invoice under GST. A person who is not registered cannot issue a GST tax invoice or collect GST. A composition taxpayer or a registered supplier making exempt supplies generally issues a bill of supply instead.
What details should a GST tax invoice contain?
Rule 46 of the CGST Rules prescribes particulars including supplier name, address and GSTIN; a consecutive invoice number and date; recipient details; HSN/SAC and description; quantity and unit for goods; total and taxable value; tax rate and tax amount; place of supply and state code for inter-state supplies; reverse-charge status; and supplier signature or digital signature, subject to applicable exceptions.
When should I use CGST and SGST instead of IGST?
A domestic intra-state supply is generally taxed with CGST plus SGST or UTGST. An inter-state supply is generally taxed with IGST. The legally determined place of supply and supplier location decide the treatment; the recipient's address alone may not determine it for every service.
How many HSN or SAC digits are required?
For GSTR-1 Table 12 reporting, the GSTN advisory generally requires at least 4 HSN digits where preceding-year aggregate annual turnover is up to ₹5 crore and 6 digits where it is above ₹5 crore. The correct classification and any invoice-specific requirement should be verified using the GST portal's HSN/SAC search.
Is a printable PDF the same as an e-invoice?
No. An e-invoice under GST is a covered invoice reported to an authorised Invoice Registration Portal and returned with an Invoice Reference Number (IRN) and signed QR code. This generator only formats a printable invoice and does not create an IRN or QR code.
Who is generally covered by e-invoicing?
The general threshold is aggregate turnover exceeding ₹5 crore in any preceding financial year from 2017–18 onward, based on PAN, subject to notified exclusions and the documents/supplies covered by the rules. Check the current e-invoice portal and notifications for your category.
Which GST rate should I select?
The rate depends on the exact HSN/SAC classification, description, conditions and current rate notification. The two main rates are 5% and 18%, with a special 40% rate for selected supplies and other notified treatments. Verify the rate in the current CBIC rate schedule before issuing an invoice.
