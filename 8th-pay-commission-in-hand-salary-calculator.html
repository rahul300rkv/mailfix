How this calculator works

Estimate gross and take-home pay with separate DA, HRA, transport, NPS and deduction assumptions. The result uses only your inputs and does not contain a hidden forecast or official future pay table.

Calculation boundaries

Where the final Commission formula is not notified, the result is illustrative. This tool does not decide eligibility for allowances, pension or service benefits. Gratuity uses the Payment of Gratuity Act formula for eligible employees; separate government service rules may apply.

Use the result responsibly

  • Keep basic pay or pension separate from allowances.
  • Use scenario factors only as modelling inputs, not official recommendations.
  • Change DA, HRA, NPS, deductions and months independently where available.
  • Recalculate after formal notification and verify rules for your category.
Actual revised pay, pension, allowances and arrears depend on Commission recommendations and Government orders. This is not an official Government of India service.

Frequently asked questions

How is estimated take-home pay calculated?

The tool adds scenario basic, DA, HRA and allowances, then subtracts the entered NPS contribution and other deductions.

Does it calculate NPS correctly for every employee?

No. It applies your chosen percentage to scenario basic plus DA as an illustration; actual contribution rules depend on coverage and orders.

Is income tax included?

Only if you include a tax estimate in the other monthly deductions field.
