Home Loan Tax Benefit Calculator

Estimate eligible interest and principal deductions under the old and new tax regimes for FY 2025–26 (AY 2026–27).

Estimate potential home-loan tax benefits using your annual interest, principal repayment and ownership share. This tool covers FY 2025–26 / AY 2026–27 under the Income-tax Act, 1961 only; it is not a full tax-return calculator.

1. Loan and property details
Enter amounts attributable to you. Ownership share is applied to the interest and principal entered. The calculator does not allocate deductions between co-owners.
2. Estimated benefit

How the estimate works

  • Self-occupied interest: eligible purchase/construction loans under the old regime may qualify up to ₹2 lakh under section 24(b), subject to conditions. Other cases may be limited to ₹30,000. No self-occupied interest deduction is available under the new regime.
  • Principal repayment: eligible principal can be included in the combined ₹1.5 lakh section 80C limit under the old regime. It is not available under the new regime.
  • Let-out property: interest is considered in computing house-property income. Under the old regime, house-property loss set-off against other heads is capped at ₹2 lakh. Under the new regime, house-property loss cannot be set off against other heads.

Sources: Income Tax Department: deductions and regimes · Self-occupied house property.

Scope and limitations

This is a simplified estimate. It does not calculate full tax liability, slab boundaries, rebate, surcharge, exact rental income, pre-construction interest, stamp duty, co-owner eligibility or all statutory conditions. The selected marginal rate is used only for an indicative saving.

From 1 April 2026, the Income-tax Act, 2025 uses “Tax Year” for FY 2026–27 onwards. This tool is limited to FY 2025–26 / AY 2026–27 under the 1961 Act. Read the Income Tax Department's new Act FAQ.

FAQs

Can I claim both interest and principal?

Under the old regime, eligible interest may qualify under section 24(b), while principal may form part of the combined section 80C limit.

Can I claim self-occupied interest in the new regime?

No. The self-occupied home-loan interest deduction under section 24(b) is not available in the new regime.

Is the 80C limit only for home loans?

No. It is a combined limit for several eligible investments and payments.
